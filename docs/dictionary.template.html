Every symbol the library draws, what it means, and when to reach for it.

Every symbol here is one of three things.

  • NodeA place that has a temperature.
  • PathA route heat takes between two places.
  • SourceHeat arriving from outside the network.

A box is a resistance: heat crosses it and drops in temperature. An arrow is a rate.
